How to reach Pawna Lake

Pawna Lake sits between Pune and Lonavala in Maharashtra. Here's how to get to the camp.

directions_car

From Pune

Drive towards Lonavala on the old Mumbai–Pune highway, turn off at Kamshet and follow the road to Pawna Lake. Around 2 hours by car.

directions_car

From Mumbai

Take the Mumbai–Pune Expressway to Lonavala, then head towards Pawna Lake. Around 3 hours by car.

train

By train

The nearest stations are Lonavala and Kamshet. From there, take a local cab or rickshaw to the camp.
