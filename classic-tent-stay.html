About this stay

Our Classic Tent Stay is the simple, real camping experience right by Pawna Lake. Arrive in the afternoon, settle into your tent and spend the evening by the water with snacks, tea and the sunset.

As it gets dark, the BBQ is lit, dinner is served and the bonfire and music get going. It's a great pick for friends and couples who want a fun night outdoors without spending much.
