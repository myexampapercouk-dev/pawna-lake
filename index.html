About us

Pawna Lake is our home ground.

We started Sunset Pawna for people who want to get away from the city without a long trip or complicated plans. Pack a bag, drive a couple of hours from Pune, and you're beside the lake with the hills all around.

Our job is to make the rest simple: a comfortable tent, good food, a bonfire with music, and a team that's around when you need anything.

Privacy Policy

How we handle the details you share with us.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you enquire or book, we collect the details you share with us, such as your name, phone number, travel dates and number of guests.

How we use it

We use your details only to answer your enquiry, manage your booking and contact you about your stay.

Sharing

We do not sell your details. We share them only when needed to run your booking or when required by law.

Payments

If you pay online, payment is handled by the payment provider. We do not store your card or bank details.

Terms & Conditions

Please read these before you book a stay with us.

Last updated: October 2026

Bookings

A booking is confirmed only after we confirm it on WhatsApp and receive the advance payment. Prices are per person and include the items listed for each stay.

ID proof

Every guest must carry a valid government photo ID. We may refuse check-in without it.

At the camp

  • Please respect other guests and keep noise down late at night.
  • Do not litter. Help us keep Pawna Lake clean.
  • Swimming in the lake is not allowed.
  • Illegal substances are strictly not allowed.
  • Guests are responsible for their own belongings.

Damage

Guests are responsible for any damage they cause to tents, bedding or camp property and may be charged for it.

Weather

Camping is outdoors and depends on the weather. If conditions are unsafe, we may change activities or reschedule your stay.

Right to refuse

We may refuse or end a stay if a guest's behaviour puts others at risk or breaks these terms.

Policies

Refund Policy

How cancellations, date changes and refunds work.

Last updated: October 2026

Changing your date

Need a different date? Message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we'll try to move your booking, subject to availability.

Cancellations

To cancel, message us on WhatsApp at +91 93733 28374. The refund you receive depends on how far ahead you cancel:

  • 7 or more days before check-in: full refund of the advance.
  • 3 to 6 days before check-in: 50% refund of the advance.
  • Less than 3 days before check-in: no refund, but we will try to move your booking to another date.

If we cancel

If we have to cancel because of weather, safety or other reasons outside your control, we will offer a new date or a full refund of your advance.

No-shows

If you don't arrive on your booked date without telling us, the advance is not refundable.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 7 working days.
