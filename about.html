About us

Lakeside camping, done simply

Sunset Pawna is a lakeside campsite at Pawna Lake near Pune and Lonavala.

Our story

A getaway that feels easy

We started Sunset Pawna for people who want to get away from the city without a long trip or complicated plans. Pack a bag, drive a couple of hours, and you're beside the lake with the hills all around.

Our job is to make the rest simple: a comfortable tent, good food, a bonfire with music, and a team that's around when you need anything.
