Help

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about booking, food, activities and getting here.

Booking

How do I book?

Tap any Book button or message us on WhatsApp at +91 93733 28374 with your date and number of guests. We'll confirm availability and share payment details.

What are the prices?

Classic Tent Stay is ₹1,000 per person and White Triangle is ₹2,000 per person. Both include evening snacks and tea, limited BBQ, unlimited dinner, bonfire and music.

Do you take group bookings?

Yes. Friends, families, college groups and office teams are welcome. Message us with your group size for availability.

At the camp

What food is included?

Evening snacks and tea, a limited BBQ and an unlimited dinner. Tell us in advance if anyone in your group has food preferences.

What activities are there?

Bonfire and music, cricket, badminton, archery, darts, carrom and chess. Boating is available at extra cost, and there are treks nearby.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is in the afternoon and check-out is the next morning. We'll confirm exact timings when you book.

Is it safe for families and couples?

Yes. Families, couples and groups all stay with us. Our team is on site through your stay.

Before you go

What should I carry?

Comfortable clothes, a light jacket for the night, a torch, any medicines you need, ID proof for each guest and a power bank.

How do I reach the camp?

See our How to Reach page. We'll also share the exact location pin on WhatsApp after booking.

Can I cancel or change my date?

Please see our Cancellation Policy, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll help.

Still have a question?

We usually reply quickly on WhatsApp.
